# NNNN. Short title in the imperative or as a statement

Status: proposed | accepted

## Context

What was true that forced a choice. The constraint, the pressure, the requirement, or the thing that stopped working. Write this so it still makes sense to someone who has never seen the alternative — including you, in a year.

## Decision

What was chosen, stated plainly and in the present tense.

Lead with the concrete — the bootloader, the filesystem, the packages, the services, the files. Tables beat prose for an inventory. Reasoning about why this and not the obvious alternative comes *after* it: a reader asking "what did we do?" should not have to derive the answer from a chain of implications.
